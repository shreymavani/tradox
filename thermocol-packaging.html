Our Product Range

From loose fill and sheets to custom moulded trays — EPS solutions for food, pharma, textile, and industrial packing

EPS Beads & Loose Fill

Virgin and recycled expanded polystyrene beads for void fill, bean bags, and cushioning around packed goods.

  • • Food-grade and industrial grades
  • • Uniform bead size for consistent fill
  • • Lightweight — cuts freight cost
  • • Supplied in bags; bulk quantities on request

Thermocol Sheets & Blanks

Cut-to-size EPS sheets, blocks, and simple shapes for insulation, prototyping, and protective layering.

  • • Multiple thicknesses and densities
  • • Clean edges — sheets, stars, discs, and custom cuts
  • • Ideal for lining cartons and cold boxes
  • • Volume pricing for repeat orders

Blocks, Cylinders & Craft EPS

Pre-cut geometric thermocol for displays, school projects, floral bases, and lightweight structural mock-ups.

  • • Cubes, cylinders, arches, and spheres
  • • High-density options for sharper edges
  • • White finish ready to paint or wrap
  • • Custom profiles on request

Insulated Thermocol Boxes

Thick-wall EPS shippers for perishables — vegetables, seafood, and dairy — with optional printed handling panels.

  • • Excellent thermal retention in transit
  • • Stackable lids with handle cut-outs
  • • Food-contact grades available
  • • Custom inner dimensions and print

Fragile Goods Moulded Sets

Engineered cavities for mirrors, glass, frames, and decor — each piece locked so nothing shifts in the truck.

  • • Mirror, panel, and odd-shape slots
  • • Top and base moulds for full enclosure
  • • Works with outer corrugated or thermocol shell
  • • Designed from your product dimensions

Custom Moulded Trays & Cavities

Grid trays, component nests, and deep shells for electronics, spares kits, and multi-SKU packing lines.

  • • Single or multi-cavity layouts
  • • High-density EPS for repeated handling
  • • Pairs with outer box and tape/seal
  • • Tooling support for new SKUs

Textile Yarn & Spool Packaging

Moulded sets for yarn spools and cones — base plates, side supports, and U-view windows for warehouse checks.

  • • Circular seats prevent roll movement
  • • Export-ready nest and lid combinations
  • • Sized for standard textile spool diameters
  • • Optional viewing cut-out in outer shell

Cone & Thread Cone Packaging

Circular-cut trays and blocks that hold sewing cones upright through packing, palletising, and shipping.

  • • Tray + separator kits for cone lines
  • • Compatible with cone winding operations
  • • Bundled with packing tape workflows
  • • Repeatable cavity spacing for automation

Laboratory & Sample Trays

Vacutainer and vial grids for diagnostics, pharma sampling, and cold-chain specimen movement.

  • • Fixed-hole grids for standard tube sizes
  • • Stable hand-off between lab stations
  • • Clean white EPS — easy to inspect
  • • Custom hole count and layout

Ribbon Reel Cores & Spools

Lightweight foam cores for ribbon, film, and tape rolls — consistent diameter, ready for winding.

  • • Smooth bore for spindle mounting
  • • Multiple widths and wall thicknesses
  • • Bulk supply for converters and gift-wrap lines
  • • Can pair with outer protective cartons
