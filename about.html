Our Story

Tradox Global started small and grew the slow, honest way — one satisfied customer at a time. Fifteen-plus years on, we supply 500+ businesses across the country, and a good number of them have been with us for most of that journey.

Working out of Ahmedabad, we focus on four things and try to do them properly:

  • Corrugated Boxes Manufacturing: Custom-designed corrugated packaging solutions tailored to your specific requirements
  • Thermocol Packaging: Superior thermal insulation and protective packaging for temperature-sensitive and fragile items
  • Paper Products Supply: Kraft, copier, duplex, FBB, and tissue supplied to exact GSM for print and packaging lines
  • Monocarton Printing: Retail-ready printed folding cartons in 4-layer and 6-layer builds for branded packaging
