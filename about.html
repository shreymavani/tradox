Our Story

Tradox Global has been serving businesses with premium packaging and fastening solutions for over 15 years. What started as a small venture has grown into a trusted name in the industry, serving 500+ satisfied customers across various sectors.

Located in Ahmedabad, we specialize in three core areas:

  • Corrugated Boxes Manufacturing: Custom-designed corrugated packaging solutions tailored to your specific requirements
  • Thermocol Packaging: Superior thermal insulation and protective packaging for temperature-sensitive and fragile items
  • Screws & Fasteners Trading: Comprehensive range of high-quality fastening solutions for industrial and commercial applications
