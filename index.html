What we supply

Four divisions, one supplier

Packaging, paper, and print lines run under one roof, so a single order covers what your line actually needs.

Division 01

Corrugated Boxes

Strong, custom-fit corrugated packaging for food & beverage, e-commerce, healthcare, and heavy industrial loads.

  • Custom designs and sizes
  • High-strength 3-ply to 7-ply board
  • Recyclable, eco-friendly stock
  • Fast turnaround time

Division 02

Thermocol Packaging

Moulded EPS inserts and cold-chain packaging that keep temperature-sensitive, fragile, and electronic goods intact in transit.

  • Excellent thermal insulation
  • Lightweight & shock-absorbing
  • Custom moulding to your part
  • Cost-effective at volume
Explore thermocol →
Division 03

Paper Products

Mill-fresh kraft, copier, duplex, FBB, and tissue — reels and reams supplied to exact GSM for print and packaging lines.

  • Kraft, copier, duplex, FBB & tissue
  • Consistent GSM, tight tolerance
  • Virgin & recycled grades
  • Reel, ream & pallet quantities
Explore paper →
Division 04

Monocarton

Retail-ready printed folding cartons in 4-layer and 6-layer builds for pharma, FMCG, cosmetics, and food brands.

  • 4-layer & 6-layer constructions
  • Offset print with premium finishes
  • Custom die-cutting & windows
  • Food & pharma-grade board
