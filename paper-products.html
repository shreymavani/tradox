Our Product Range

Five core paper and board grades, each specified for its job

📜

Kraft Paper

Strong virgin & recycled kraft reels for corrugation liners, sacks, wrapping, and paper bags.

  • • GSM: 70–300 (liner 100–200)
  • • Burst Factor: 16–35 BF
  • • Natural, golden & bleached shades
  • • High tensile & tear strength
📄

Copier Paper

Bright, jam-free multipurpose paper for printers, copiers, and everyday office use.

  • • GSM: 70 / 75 / 80
  • • Brightness 92–104%, CIE ~167
  • • A4, A3, Legal & Letter sizes
  • • 500 sheets/ream, 100% wood pulp
🗂️

Duplex Board

Coated white-top duplex board for cartons, retail boxes, and cost-effective packaging.

  • • GSM: 230–450
  • • Grey-back & white-back options
  • • Caliper ~0.30–0.60 mm
  • • Smooth, print-ready top coat
🎴

FBB (Folding Box Board)

Premium multi-ply cartonboard with high bulk and stiffness for high-end print.

  • • GSM: 200–400
  • • Caliper ~350–650 microns
  • • Food-grade, bright white surface
  • • Ideal for pharma & cosmetics cartons
🧻

Tissue Paper

Soft, absorbent tissue for facial, napkin, toilet, and kitchen-towel converting.

  • • GSM: 12–45
  • • 1, 2 & 3-ply options
  • • Virgin & recycled grades
  • • Food-safe, lint-free
