Our Product Range

4-Layer Monocarton

Special pharma box and everyday product packaging — the same offset-quality cartons we supply for pharma, agriculture, cosmetics, FMCG, and electronics.

  • • Pharma box & general packaging material cartons
  • • Board: 250–350 GSM (Duplex / FBB / SBS)
  • • 4-ply build — offset print, gloss or matte lamination
  • • Die-cut, foldable, glue-ready for retail

6-Layer Monocarton

Premium square cartons for brands that need a heavier feel on shelf — with the specialty board options our catalogue lists for high-end packs.

  • • Metallic box, drip-off box & UV-emboss finishes
  • • Board: 300–400 GSM + barrier plies
  • • 6-ply rigidity for stacking and transit
  • • Moisture, aroma & light protection
