Our Product Range

Two carton constructions matched to how your product ships and sells

4-Layer Monocarton

Multi-layer printed carton for everyday retail — pharma, FMCG, cosmetics, and electronics.

  • • Board: 250–350 GSM (Duplex/FBB/SBS)
  • • 4-ply laminated construction
  • • Offset print + gloss/matte lamination
  • • Die-cut, foldable, glue-ready

6-Layer Monocarton

Heavier, barrier-enhanced carton for premium and moisture-sensitive products.

  • • Board: 300–400 GSM + barrier plies
  • • 6-ply build for extra rigidity
  • • Moisture, aroma & light protection
  • • Premium finishes — UV, foil, emboss
